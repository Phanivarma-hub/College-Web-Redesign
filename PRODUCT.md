# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Prospective students, current students, parents, and alumni. Their context is searching for practical, reliable academic information, career development opportunities, and admissions guidelines, often on mobile devices or in varied ambient light settings. They need clarity, ease of navigation, and a sense of institutional security.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a modern, trustworthy, and clear portal for Smt. B. Seetha Polytechnic (SBSP). It details academic departments, calendars, examinations, results, and student development to instill confidence and trust in potential students and parents.

## Brand Personality
Practical · Empowering · Trustworthy

## Anti-references
- **Government Website Aesthetic**: Thin borders, tiny fonts, dense update blocks, disorganized PDF dumps, and dry "Notice Board" designs.
- **Corporate SaaS Startup Look**: Excessive glowing gradients, abstract blobs, tech-bro marketing jargon like "Revolutionizing Education," and floating 3D shapes.
- **Cyberpunk / Neon Styling**: Heavy glow effects, hacker vibes, electric blue/magenta gradients (#00FFFF/#FF00FF).
- **Ultra-Minimal Luxury Design**: Excessively tiny text, hidden menus, huge empty spacing that makes finding practical college calendar and syllabus information difficult.
- **Vibecoded Demos**: Hyper-stylized, overly flashy motion layouts that sacrifice clarity for visual novelty.

## Design Principles
- **Readability & Trust First**: Every layout decision prioritizes reading comfort, high contrast, and logical layout structure.
- **Academic Professionalism**: Use clean, modern layouts that feel established, technical, and stable.
- **Authentic Over Fake**: Prioritize real campus imagery, honest statistics, and plain-english copy over generic stock photography and corporate buzzwords.
- **Structured Information Hierarchy**: Information is structured in readable cards, clean lists, and distinct sections with consistent left alignment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Typography Sizing**: Body text target of 16px-18px minimum to ensure readability.
- **WCAG compliance**: High text-to-background contrast (target ≥ 4.5:1), avoiding low-contrast muted grays.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respect system motion preferences and keep scroll animations functional and non-distracting.
- **Soft Contrast**: Avoid harsh pure black (#000000) and pure white (#FFFFFF) to reduce eye strain, using soft neutrals instead.
