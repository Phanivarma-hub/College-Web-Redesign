Procedure for Admission into the Diploma Courses
Selection of candidates is governed by the Rules and Regulations laid down in this regard from time to time by Government of Andhra Pradesh.

Candidates who wish to seek admission in any of the Diploma courses will have to appear for the Common Entrance Test for admissions into Polytechnics (POLYCET) conducted by the State Board of Technical Education and Training, Andhra Pradesh, Mangalagiri. Only the candidates satisfying the following requirements will be eligible to appear for the Common Entrance Test for admissions into Polytechnics (POLYCET).

The candidates seeking admission should have appeared for S.S.C examination, conducted by the Board of Secondary Education, Andhra Pradesh or equivalent examination thereto, at the time of applying for the Common Entrance Test for admissions into Polytechnics (POLYCET). In case of candidates whose results of their Qualifying Examinations is pending, their selection shall be subject to production of proof of their passing the qualifying examination in one attempt or compartmentally at the time of admission.

Admissions are made based on the merit obtained in the Common Entrance Test (POLYCET) and the reservation rules stipulated by the Government of Andhra Pradesh from time to time.

Duration and pattern of the courses
All the Diploma programs run at Smt. B. Seetha Polytechnic are of AICTE approved 3 years duration of academic instruction. All the Diploma courses are run on year wise pattern in the first year, and the remaining two years are run in the semester pattern.

Medium of Instruction
The medium of instruction and examination shall be in English.

Fee Reimbursement Details
Candidates with a total family income of ₹2.50 lakhs or less are eligible for fee exemption. Under this exemption, the Government of Andhra Pradesh will pay the college tuition fee directly to the institution on behalf of the candidate.

Refer to G.O. Ms. No. 115, Social Welfare (Edn.) Department, dated 30-11-2019, for further details.

Scholarship Applications
Scholarships are linked to Aadhaar for cash transfer.
Students must provide their Aadhaar numbers as per Govt. Memo No. 16635/sw. Edn. 2/2012-4, dated 29-12-2012.
Candidates exempted from fee payment must apply for fee reimbursement online (via Jnanabhumi website).
Government orders at the time of admission will be applicable.
Scholarships for Diploma Programs
a) AP State Government Scholarship
Various schemes are available for eligible students under the AP State Government’s scholarship initiatives, covering tuition fees, maintenance allowances, and other academic expenses.

b) Government of India (GOI) Scholarships
Numerous national-level scholarship schemes are available to support students pursuing technical and diploma programs.

Pragati Scholarship
This flagship government initiative by AICTE supports meritorious girl students pursuing technical education.

Eligibility: Girl students enrolled in AICTE-approved institutions for diploma or degree courses.
Benefits: ₹50,000 per annum for up to 3 years (maximum).
Saksham Scholarship
Introduced by the Ministry of Education (MoE), implemented by AICTE, this scholarship encourages specially-abled students to pursue technical education.

Eligibility: Specially-abled students with a disability level of 40% or more, enrolled in AICTE-approved diploma programs.
Benefits: ₹50,000 per annum for up to 3 years, covering tuition fees, books, stationery, equipment, and more. (Hostel and medical charges not included.)
Swanath Scholarship
This AICTE scholarship supports students who have lost parents or are wards of martyred armed forces personnel.

Eligibility: Students who lost either/both parents or wards of armed forces personnel martyred in action, enrolled in AICTE-approved institutions.
Benefits: ₹50,000 per annum as lump sum to cover tuition fees, books, equipment, and academic expenses.
Objective: To ensure no deserving student is deprived of technical education due to personal tragedies.
Yashasvi Scholarship
This AICTE scheme encourages students to pursue technical education in core engineering branches like Civil, Electrical, Electronics & Communication, and Mechanical Engineering.

Benefits: ₹50,000 per annum for every year of study (up to 4 years) for students admitted in the first year.
